Privacy Policy for TestPilot

Effective Date: October 2023

1. Information We Collect

TestPilot is designed to help developers find testers for their Android applications. We collect your Google Account information (Email, Name) when you sign in, as well as the app information (package name, descriptions, links) that you submit for testing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to manage reciprocal testing cohorts, track testing activity (e.g., daily check-ins), and ensure compliance with our testing rules. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for authentication and database management. By using TestPilot, you also agree to Firebase's privacy policies. Your app details will be shared with other developers in your cohort to facilitate the testing process.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time from the "Settings" menu within the app. Deleting your account will remove your app submissions and testing history.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app's support channel.
